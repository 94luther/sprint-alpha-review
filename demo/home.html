Deliberately missing

There are no star ratings on this screen.

Every app in your reference shows 4.7 with 1,500 reviews. Sprint has never collected a single rating, so putting stars here would be inventing the one number a customer trusts most.

What is shown instead is what Sprint can actually prove: whether the shop is open, how long the delivery takes, and what it costs. When real ratings exist, they go here.

Real numbers only

Two prices, and two blanks.

A parcel is P87.00 for half a kilo and P93.00 for a kilo across Gaborone, VAT and fuel already in.

The grocery and liquor delivery fees say fee to confirm because nobody has set them. A guess on this screen becomes a promise to a customer.

Why collect is a real tab

You have 57 counters. They do not.

Wanzy and Zebras have no branch outside Gaborone. Sprint has 30 offices and 27 service points with an address and a phone number each, plus 3 in South Africa.

Collect is not a lesser option here. It is the thing nobody else can offer.
