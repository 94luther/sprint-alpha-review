## Nothing expires inside sixty days

The window to 11 November 2026 is clear. Not one licence, registration or certificate in the table above runs out before the pilot goes live on 9 November.

**But two dates sit just past it and both matter.**

- **The PPRA codes expire 19 January 2027**, four days after the decision date on this project. Renewal starts in November, not January, or the company walks into tender season unregistered again.
- **The tax clearance expires 20 March 2027.** Routine, but every tender asks for it.

## The finding that matters more than any date

**Sprint has no insurance document on this machine at all.** Not goods in transit, not motor or fleet, not public liability, not professional indemnity, not workers compensation. The document scanner categorised fourteen files as insurance and every one of them is a broker's waybill, a spreadsheet or an email, not a policy.

This brick cannot be laid on the strength of the four rows above, because the app is asking to carry medicines and liquor and nobody can say what the current cover excludes. An ordinary goods in transit policy very often excludes pharmaceuticals, excludes liquor, or caps single consignment value in a way that a phone or a prescription breaches.

The same is true of the fleet. There is no vehicle list with registration numbers, no road transport permits, no motor insurance schedule and no driver licence records anywhere on this machine.

## One thing worth knowing about the competition

The BOCRA register lists 31 licensed commercial postal operators. **Wanzy is not among them**, and neither is Nexus Innovations, the company that develops it. Zebras Delivery does not appear under that name either. There is a licensed courier called Zebra Hub of Excellence with a licence to 6 March 2028, but nothing on this machine says it is the same business as the Zebras Delivery app, so it is recorded here as an open question and not as a fact.

This is not an accusation. A food delivery app may sit outside the postal regime entirely. It is a question worth asking BOCRA, because the moment those apps carry a parcel rather than a meal, the answer starts to matter, and Sprint is the one holding the licence.

## What must happen before the pilot

1. Read the goods in transit policy and its exclusions. Until then the medicine lane is not insured, whatever BoMRA says.
2. Get the BOCRA licence Annexure 1 and read the conditions.
3. Build the vehicle and driver list with expiry dates, because the director sweep asks where every vehicle is and nobody can answer.
4. Diarise the PPRA renewal for early November 2026.

## How this register stays true

It is rebuilt from originals, never from itself. Any row whose date came from a scan carries the word rendered, because a scan that cannot be read is a date nobody has checked. A file existing is not a file being current, and that mistake already cost a tender once.
