The thing nobody else separates

Where the money goes

No rider waits at a counter for free.

Sending a rider early wins minutes and costs a rider standing about. So it is allowed only where a shop has earned it by being fast over twenty real orders, measured rather than agreed in a meeting. A shop can lose it the same way.

And a till Sprint installed and failed to keep working is recorded as Sprint's fault, not the shop's, so nobody is penalised for our hardware.
