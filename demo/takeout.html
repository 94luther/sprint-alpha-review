The market that matters

Spur, Bull and Bush, The Pond are already online.

The chains and the sit down restaurants are on Wanzy and Zebras already. Fighting for them is fighting for the part of the market somebody else has built. The braai spot, the home kitchen, the tuckshop and the lunch counter have no app at all, and a printer at the fire plus a rider network is exactly what they cannot build for themselves.

No real business is named until it signs. Each tile is a kind of kitchen, in a colour, and the real name and logo drop in the day the first one does.

The Sprint OS theme

Same colours the office already lives in.

Deep green into amber, glass panels, white pill buttons with dark green type. Lifted directly from Sprint OS so the customer app and the office console read as one company.

The plain white version is one link away if the IT department prefers it.

Real numbers only

Every fee says to confirm.

No takeout delivery fee has been set anywhere. Parcels have a signed rate card, P87.00 for half a kilo, but a hot meal across town is a different price and it is Barbara's cost lines that set it. Until then the screen refuses to guess.

The time promise is real: 45 minutes, and the shop must accept within two minutes.

Still missing

Ratings, photos, and a single signed restaurant.

No star ratings, because Sprint has never collected one. No food photographs, because there are none on file. And no restaurant has agreed to anything yet, so every card here is a brand on the street, not a merchant on the platform. Signing the first five is brick 39.
